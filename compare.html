Who each one fits

DetailerLogic

You run one truck or a small shop, you price by the car in front of you, and you'd rather we set it up than spend a weekend on it.

Weak spot: no native iPhone or Android app yet, and no crew payroll.

OrbisX

You run a staffed shop that sells retail product, tracks inventory and pays technicians by the hour or commission.

A solo operator pays the same price for modules a one-truck business won't use.

DetailerMade

You want the cheapest entry plan, a native phone app, and you're comfortable setting everything up yourself.

Crews, payroll and quality checklists sit on the $99 and $199 plans.

What we built differently

  • Inside and outside condition, priced separatelyA trashed interior doesn't raise the price of a clean exterior. Each part of the job is priced on its own condition.
  • Your real hourly rate drives every quoteSet what your time is worth once. Sizes and condition scale from there, so a 6-hour job never goes out at a 2-hour price.
  • A booking page with live pricesClients pick the vehicle, the services and the condition, and see the price before they book. Travel charge by ZIP code if you want it.
  • Rain warnings on booked mobile jobsThe app flags jobs with rain in the forecast and writes the reschedule text for you.
  • Rebooking remindersA list of past clients who are due again, with the text ready to send.
  • Built by a working detailerImpact Detailing, Bensalem PA. 31 years in the trade. Every support email is answered by the detailer who built it.
