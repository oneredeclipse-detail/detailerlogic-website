What each one does well

Solo and mobile detailers

DetailerLogic

You price by the car in front of you. The app grades the inside and outside on their own, runs it against your hourly rate, and gives the client a price before they book. We load your prices and past clients for you.

Where we lose: no native iPhone or Android app yet, no crew logins until Shop launches, no inventory, payroll or dent-repair estimating.

Crews in any trade

Jobber

The big name in field service software. Scheduling, dispatch, routing and QuickBooks for teams, with online booking on every plan.

The catch for a detailer: it's built for every trade, so nothing in it knows vehicle size or condition. Automated reminders and two-way texting start on Connect at $139 a month.

PPF, wrap, tint, coatings

Urable

Built around vehicle work, with unlimited users on every plan and a 3D visualizer for film and wrap jobs. Strong value once you have a team.

The catch for a solo detailer: online booking starts on Pro at $110 a month, not on the $70 plan.

Dent repair and recon

Mobile Tech RX

The deepest estimating on this page for dent repair, dealer and recon work, priced by service module.

The catch for a detailer: a 5-day trial, deposits on estimates start at $99 a month, and customer self-booking isn't listed on any plan.

Staffed shops

OrbisX

One plan with everything in it: inventory, technician time tracking, QuickBooks and unlimited users. The longest free trial here at 30 days.

The catch for a solo detailer: you pay the same $100 a month as a ten-person shop, for modules a one-truck business won't open.

Lowest entry price

DetailerMade

The cheapest way in at $19 a month, with a booking page, payments and team management on that plan.

The catch: custom services and deposits start on the $99 plan, and the trial is 7 days.

Straight answers

Is DetailerLogic a Jobber alternative for detailers?

For a solo or mobile detailer, yes. Jobber is built for crews in many trades and does that well. DetailerLogic is built for one job: pricing a vehicle by size and condition, then booking and rebooking the client. If you run several crews and need dispatch and routing, Jobber is the better fit.

Which detailing app is cheapest?

DetailerMade's Essential plan at $19 a month. Ours starts at $29.95. Check what the entry plan includes before you compare: on several apps here, online booking or custom services start on a higher plan.

Which one has the longest free trial?

OrbisX at 30 days. Ours and Jobber's are 14 days with no card. DetailerMade is 7 days and Mobile Tech RX is 5.

Do I have to set DetailerLogic up myself?

No. Send a photo of your price list and a screenshot or Square export of your past clients. We load both within 24 hours, exactly as you charge now.
