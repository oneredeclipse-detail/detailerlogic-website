Straight answers.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. You get 14 days of everything in Pro without a card. Pick a plan when you're ready, or don't.

I already have prices. Do I start over?

No. Send a photo of your price list, a link to your site or Facebook, or just your 3 most common jobs. We load them within 24 hours, exactly as you charge now.

iPhone or Android?

Both. It runs in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app.

How do card payments work?

You connect your own Stripe account and the money goes straight to you. Our fee is 1% on Weekend or 0.5% on Pro, and it's taken from the job amount only. We never take a cut of the tip. Stripe's own processing fee applies to the whole card charge, the same as any card reader.

Where does the suggested hourly rate come from?

From the income level of your ZIP code and how long you've been detailing. It's a starting point. You set your own rate and can change it any time.

What happens when it rains?

The app checks the 7-day forecast against your booked mobile jobs and flags any day at 40% rain or more. One tap writes the reschedule text, and your phone can warn you the night before.

Can I cancel?

Any time, no contract. The Founding guarantee stands on top of that: 3 paid jobs in 60 days or every dollar back, if you did your part.

What do you do with my data?

Your clients and jobs are yours, visible only to your account. Google Calendar sync only touches the events the app creates. Privacy policy · How we handle Google data
